**Topics:**
- Motivation: decision-making under uncertainty
- From Bayesianism to quasi-Bayesianism and infra-Bayesianism
- Convex sets of probability measures and their representations
- Connections to other imprecise probabilistic models, including lower previsions and sets of desirable gambles
- Learning and reasoning with credal sets, including notions of independence and conditional independence

**Suggested readings:**
- [Introduction to the Theory of Sets of Probabilities](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~qbayes/Tutorial/quasi-bayesian.html) by Fabio Cozman
- [Introduction to the Theory of Imprecise Probability](https://pure.tue.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/356224229/978-3-030-83640-5_3.pdf) by Erik Quaeghebeur
- [SIPTA School 2024: Introduction to Imprecise Probabilities](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gyDqpSY5B5s) by Erik Quaeghebeur
