Lectures take place on Fridays, 10 a.m. – 12 p.m., followed by the exercise
session from 12 p.m. – 2 p.m. The course is organised in two parts:
**Part I: Foundations of Imprecise Probabilities** (Lectures 2–6) and
**Part II: Learning with Imprecise Probabilities** (Lectures 7–13).
