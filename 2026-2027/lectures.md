Probabilities** (Lectures 2–6) and **Part II: Learning with Imprecise
Probabilities** (Lectures 7–13). Dates are listed on the
[schedule]({{ '/2026-2027/schedule/' | relative_url }}). Slides are uploaded
before each class; until then, the corresponding slides from the
[2025/26 offering]({{ '/2025-2026/lectures/' | relative_url }}) give an
impression of the material.
